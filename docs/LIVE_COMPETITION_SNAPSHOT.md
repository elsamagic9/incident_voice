## Submission-readiness comparison only

The public lablab pages for [SAUTI](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/assemblyai-voice-agent-hackathon/kisii-unversity-code-union/sauti-ai-voice-to-action), [Siberia](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/assemblyai-voice-agent-hackathon/frantal-company/siberia-voice-agent), and [KiaOra](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/assemblyai-voice-agent-hackathon/shinydatatech/kiaora-dispatch) each expose a project description plus links labelled **Github**, **Presentation**, and **Demo**. That demonstrates a visible, packaged submission; it does **not** verify that their demos function, that their pitch videos meet event rules, or that their implementation claims are true.

IncidentVoice has a [public GitHub repository](https://github.com/elsamagic9/incident_voice), prepared [PDF deck](IncidentVoice-Presentation.pdf), and grounded [submission copy](SUBMISSION_COPY.md). Its own [submission checklist](SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md) still marks a hosted HTTPS demo, deployed microphone rehearsal, actual demo video, final deck screenshots, and lablab form submission as pending. IncidentVoice was **not in the displayed top ten**, and a targeted public search did not surface an IncidentVoice lablab entry as of this snapshot. That is **not proof no private draft or unindexed entry exists**; only the owner’s logged-in team/submission page can confirm status. The user previously reported having no lablab team/submission link.

The [general lablab guide](https://lablab.ai/guide/ai-hackathons) describes a complete submission as an accessible prototype URL, a pitch video of at most five minutes in MP4 format, a PDF slide deck, and a public GitHub repository. These are general guidance, not independently verified event-specific form fields. Its [judging guide](https://lablab.ai/guide/how-to-win-an-ai-hackathon) emphasizes a deployed demo and a concise presentation. On the evidence visible today, **the competitive gap is submission packaging and live verifiability, not a reason to change IncidentVoice’s product concept**. The next external gate remains owner-controlled deployment and recording; no deployment or submission action was taken for this research.
