# SAUTI AI vs IncidentVoice — public evidence review

Checked **September 23, 2026**. SAUTI source snapshot: [`7d536c6`](https://github.com/Hyacinth18-creator/SAUTI-AI-KE/tree/7d536c675ccac7cfad3ebb96b040b37bd80e9556). IncidentVoice source snapshot: [`58e6991`](https://github.com/elsamagic9/incident_voice/tree/58e69918490e250540d3d95159284be405c5294d). This is a comparison of public submissions and code, **not** a test of either project's live microphone flow or a prediction of judging results.

## What SAUTI has already shipped publicly

- [SAUTI's lablab submission](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/assemblyai-voice-agent-hackathon/kisii-unversity-code-union/sauti-ai-voice-to-action) is a clearly scoped civic-reporting story: residents speak about a problem, answer follow-up questions, and receive a structured report/reference for organizations to manage. The page was created September 4 and links its [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Hyacinth18-creator/SAUTI-AI-KE), a presentation, and a [Vercel demo](https://sauti-ai-ke.vercel.app/). The demo's root returned HTTP 200 on September 23; this confirms reachability of the landing page, **not** that voice, login, or report submission works end to end.
- The [event's live board](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/assemblyai-voice-agent-hackathon/live) showed SAUTI first with 11 votes when checked. The board explicitly says **“By community vote”**; it is not a final judges' ranking and can change.
- The published [UI](https://github.com/Hyacinth18-creator/SAUTI-AI-KE/blob/7d536c675ccac7cfad3ebb96b040b37bd80e9556/src/app/page.tsx), [follow-up route](https://github.com/Hyacinth18-creator/SAUTI-AI-KE/blob/7d536c675ccac7cfad3ebb96b040b37bd80e9556/src/app/api/voice/follow-up/route.ts), and [database migration](https://github.com/Hyacinth18-creator/SAUTI-AI-KE/blob/7d536c675ccac7cfad3ebb96b040b37bd80e9556/supabase/migrations/20260904000000_create_incidents.sql) show a coherent intake-to-record concept, including incident references and status/history storage. These are genuine presentation strengths even though the live workflow was not independently exercised.

## Implications for IncidentVoice

1. **Keep the differentiation sharply demonstrable:** “A restart is not a recovery.” Show one observation-linked hypothesis, a staged action that can be canceled, explicit approval, and a before/after receipt that may still say *partial recovery*. These are the distinct capabilities supported by IncidentVoice's [README](https://github.com/elsamagic9/incident_voice/blob/58e69918490e250540d3d95159284be405c5294d/README.md), [orchestrator](https://github.com/elsamagic9/incident_voice/blob/58e69918490e250540d3d95159284be405c5294d/backend/app/services/orchestrator.py), and [SRE tools](https://github.com/elsamagic9/incident_voice/blob/58e69918490e250540d3d95159284be405c5294d/backend/app/tools/sre_tools.py). Do not compete on feature count alone.
2. **Make AssemblyAI unmistakable on camera.** A future demo should visibly capture the live managed Voice Agent or Streaming STT path from the [actual app router](https://github.com/elsamagic9/incident_voice/blob/58e69918490e250540d3d95159284be405c5294d/backend/app/api/websocket.py), while labeling simulated infrastructure effects accurately. Until such a recording exists, “integrated in source” is the defensible claim, not “proven live.”
3. **Submission completeness is the immediate competitive gap.** SAUTI's [public entry](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/assemblyai-voice-agent-hackathon/kisii-unversity-code-union/sauti-ai-voice-to-action) is already judge-accessible. IncidentVoice's [checklist](https://github.com/elsamagic9/incident_voice/blob/58e69918490e250540d3d95159284be405c5294d/docs/SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md) remains open on hosting and real-microphone video. The owner asked to defer Render, so this note does **not** authorize deployment.

## Evidence boundaries

The SAUTI [submission narrative](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/assemblyai-voice-agent-hackathon/kisii-unversity-code-union/sauti-ai-voice-to-action) and [source code](https://github.com/Hyacinth18-creator/SAUTI-AI-KE/tree/7d536c675ccac7cfad3ebb96b040b37bd80e9556) are first-party evidence of intended functionality and implemented paths; neither proves the current Vercel deployment runs the same commit or that its microphone/reporting flow succeeds. Likewise, IncidentVoice's [source](https://github.com/elsamagic9/incident_voice/tree/58e69918490e250540d3d95159284be405c5294d) and [CI](https://github.com/elsamagic9/incident_voice/actions/runs/35812413604) are not substitutes for a public live demonstration or human evaluation.
