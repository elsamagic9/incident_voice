The advertised prize pool is **$10,000: $5,000 cash plus $5,000 AssemblyAI credits**. [Event page](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/assemblyai-voice-agent-hackathon)

The public summary does not specify a mandatory product or minimum usage; IncidentVoice should visibly demonstrate its AssemblyAI speech/voice integration and name the services used. [Event page](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/assemblyai-voice-agent-hackathon)

## Platform submission guidance (general, not event-specific)

- A complete submission needs a **working prototype accessible online, a video presentation, and a pitch deck**. Lablab's broader guide also names a **public GitHub repository**, a **video of at most five minutes in MP4 format**, and a **PDF slide deck**. [Lablab guide FAQ](https://lablab.ai/guide), [AI hackathons guide](https://lablab.ai/guide/ai-hackathons)
- Lablab's submission walkthrough describes: project title (maximum 50 characters), summary (maximum 255 characters), long description (minimum 100 words), track/category and technology tags; a cover image (recommended 16:9); a video link (under 300 MB, within five minutes); GitHub URL; deployment platform; and live demo URL. These are the published *general form instructions* and should be checked against this event's actual form before final upload. [Submission walkthrough](https://lablab.ai/ai-articles/hackathon-guidelines)
- Lablab's guide says each participant must register individually and join or create a lablab team, including a solo participant. [Lablab guide FAQ](https://lablab.ai/guide)
- Lablab describes its general judging dimensions as **presentation, business value, application of technology, and originality**. Its guidance recommends a clear problem/demo/business story and a deployed, functional product with meaningful AI integration. No event-specific weights or scoring formula were visible on the event page. [Judging guidance](https://lablab.ai/guide/how-to-win-an-ai-hackathon), [AI hackathons guide](https://lablab.ai/guide/ai-hackathons)
- Lablab's general guide says open-source libraries, starter templates, and prior non-AI scaffolding are generally allowed, while most events expect the core AI functionality to be built during the event. This is **not an event-specific clearance** for any particular pre-existing IncidentVoice code. [AI hackathons guide](https://lablab.ai/guide/ai-hackathons)
